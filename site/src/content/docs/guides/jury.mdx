## Find issues to review

Open **Awards** (phone) or **Award review** (console). It has three lists for the whole session:

| List | Contains | Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To decide** | Resolved issues without an award | Oldest resolution first |
| **To apply** | Awards not yet applied, including 0 minutes and reopened issues | Oldest decision first |
| **Applied** | Resolved issues with an applied award | Latest first |

A pending award on a reopened issue shows **Reopened**.
Jury-only members see resolved issues only and cannot claim, resolve, or reopen.

## Decide

Read the description, unresolved time, resolutions, and staff notes.
Enter whole minutes and a public explanation for the team.
Zero minutes is a valid decision. Each issue gets one award; repeated submissions do not duplicate it.

On a phone, open the issue and select **Decide award**. In the console, use the form on the card.

## Confirm application

Apply the award in the external contest system first.
Then select **Confirm applied externally**. Minsky records the confirmation only.

Reopening keeps the award and its status and does not allow a second award.

## Give time to the whole contest

An outage can affect every contestant. A contest-wide award gives the same minutes to every contestant of one contest, with no exceptions.

1. Open **Award review** (console) or **Awards** (phone).
2. Under **Time awards for the whole contest**, enter whole minutes from 1 to 1440 and the reason.
3. Select **Record the decision**.

Volunteers, specialists, and jury then apply the minutes in the external contest system and select **Confirm applied externally**. Volunteers and specialists see awards that wait for confirmation above the queue.

As with an issue award, the decision and the confirmation are separate records with separate people. Minsky changes no external clock. A contestant's end time grows only by confirmed minutes. Repeating a decision or a confirmation never records it twice.

Jury members can also post session announcements. See [Post announcements](/guides/announcements/).
