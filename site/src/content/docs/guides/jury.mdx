## Find issues to review

On a phone, open **Awards**. In the console, open **Award review**. It has three lists for the whole contest:

| List | Contains | Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **To decide** | Resolved issues without an award | Oldest resolution first |
| **To apply** | Awards not yet applied, including 0 minutes and reopened issues | Oldest decision first |
| **Applied** | Resolved issues with an applied award | Latest first |

A pending award on a reopened issue shows **Reopened**.
Jury-only members see resolved issues only and cannot claim, resolve, or reopen.

## Decide an award

Read the description, unresolved time, resolutions, and staff notes.
Enter whole minutes and a public explanation for the team.
Zero minutes is a valid decision. Each issue gets one award, and repeated submissions do not duplicate it.

On a phone, open the issue and select **Decide award**. In the console, use the form on the card.

## Confirm application

1. Apply the award in the external contest system.
2. Select **Confirm applied externally**. Minsky records the confirmation only.

Reopening keeps the award and its status and does not allow a second award.

## Give time to the whole contest

An outage can affect every contestant. A contest-wide award gives the same minutes to every contestant of one contest, with no exceptions.

1. On a phone, open **Awards**. In the console, open **Award review**.
2. Under **Time awards for the whole contest**, enter whole minutes from 1 to 1440 and the reason.
3. Select **Record the decision**.

Volunteers, specialists, and jury then apply the minutes in the external contest system and select **Confirm applied externally**. Volunteers and specialists see awards that wait for confirmation above the queue.

As with an issue award, the decision and the confirmation are separate records with separate people. Minsky changes no external clock. A contestant's end time grows only by confirmed minutes. Repeating a decision or a confirmation never records it twice.

Only one award of each length can wait for confirmation in a contest. If you record 15 minutes while another 15-minute award waits, Minsky shows "An award of this length is waiting for confirmation." Your text stays in the form, and the list shows the waiting award. This happens when a response is lost and you try again, so check the list first. You can record the same length again after staff confirm the first award.

### Withdraw a wrong award

You can withdraw a contest-wide award that nobody has confirmed. Only jury can.

1. In **Time awards for the whole contest**, select **Withdraw** on the award.
2. Read the dialog. Select **Withdraw award** to confirm, or **Keep the award** to go back.

The award stays in the list as withdrawn, with your name. It gives no time, and contestants never see it. Staff are told, and the award leaves the **Time awards to confirm** card of volunteers and specialists. The length is free again, so you can record the right award.

These limits apply:

- You cannot undo a withdrawal.
- You cannot withdraw an award that staff already confirmed, because contestants may already use its minutes.
- Staff cannot confirm a withdrawn award.
- If two people act at the same moment, only one action takes effect, and the other person sees a message.
- Repeating the same withdrawal records it once.
- Issue awards have no withdrawal.

Jury members can also post contest announcements. See [Post announcements](/guides/announcements/).
