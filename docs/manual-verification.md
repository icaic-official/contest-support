1. Sign in with a username (case-insensitive), refresh and verify session restoration. Sign out and verify contest data disappears. An account without membership sees the access message. Change the password through the labeled form.
2. Revoke a session membership and verify subsequent direct database reads return no session data. Verify the UI clears data after refresh/focus. Attempt to remove the last admin: it must fail.

4. Open **Report an issue** as a contestant (or **Report for team** as support), complete the form and submit. Successful submission closes the form and selects the new report. **Cancel report** returns focus to the report button. In another signed-in browser, claim as volunteer, use **Audience** to add a public reply and an internal note, then escalate. The volunteer remains owner until another specialist accepts in the amber handoff panel. Verify the contestant cannot see the private note, including through direct API/history queries. **Send reply** remains disabled for blank input.
