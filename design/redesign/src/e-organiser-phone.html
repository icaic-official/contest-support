Minsky · Organiser app

Phone-first for organisers in the hall.

Staff open Minsky in their phone browser. One thumb reaches the main action. Sheets slide up for replies, resolutions and awards. The tabs change with the person's roles.

Preview as (mockup only)
  • Helper: volunteer and specialist. Claim, reply, escalate, accept handoffs, resolve, report for a team.
  • Jury: resolved issues only. Record awards and confirm external application.
  • Admin: helper tabs, plus a Session tab for intake, image limits and accounts.

On a phone-sized window this page shows the app full screen without the frame.
