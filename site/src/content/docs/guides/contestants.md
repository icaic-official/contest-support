Sign in from the link on your team's sign-in sheet. The link fills in your event code. See [Sign in](/guides/getting-started/).

## Use a phone or a laptop

The same page works on a phone and on a laptop.

- On a phone, three tabs stay at the bottom of the screen: **Report**, **My reports**, and **Account**. **Report** opens the report form in one tap, from any screen. **My reports** shows the contest clock, your end time, and your reports. Select a report to read it, and select **My reports** to go back. **Account** opens your account page. When reporting is closed, **Report** shows why and the clock, with no form.
- On a laptop, a window 1024 px wide or wider shows your reports on the left and the open report on the right.

When the keyboard is open on a phone, the tabs hide, and the text field and the **Send** button stay in view. Your draft stays if you turn the phone.

## Report a problem

1. Select **Report an issue**.
2. Choose a category and describe what happened.
3. Select **Send report**.

Minsky fills in your team and location. The timer starts when you send the report.
If you cannot send a report, reporting is closed. The clock above the button says why. Contact staff through the channel your organizer gives you.

## Read the contest clock

Reporting follows the contest's schedule. The clock shows times in the event's time zone. If your device uses another zone, the clock adds "Your time" with your own time.

| The clock says | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Reporting opens at 13:00. | The contest has not started. You can report from that time. |
| Ends in 1:42:10 | The contest runs. The countdown shows the time left. |
| Reporting closes in 8:00 | The contest ended, and the grace period runs. You can still report. |
| Reporting closed at 18:25. | You can no longer report. Ask staff. |
| Reporting is closed by the organizers. | Staff closed reporting by hand. |
| Reporting opens when every team has its live password. | A live contest waits until every team has its new password. Ask staff. |

Your end time is the contest's end plus the minutes that staff confirmed as applied. These minutes come from a time award on one of your issues, or from an award that the jury gave to the whole contest. The clock then says "Your end time is 18:10. It includes 10 minutes that staff confirmed as applied."
Minsky does not change the external contest clock. An award counts here only after staff confirm that they applied it there.

The page updates itself at each of these times. The update can take up to 15 seconds. The server decides whether it accepts your report, so a report that you send after the end can be refused even if the page has not yet updated.

## Follow progress

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Waiting for staff. |
| In progress | A staff member owns the issue. |
| Awaiting specialist | The owner asked for a specialist. The owner stays responsible until one accepts. |
| Resolved | Staff recorded a resolution. The timer stopped. |

Open an issue to see replies, images that staff shared with your team, resolutions, and awards.
You can reply while the issue is unresolved. You see only your team's issues.
Staff notes and staff-only images stay hidden. You cannot upload images.

## Read announcements

The **Announcements** list is at the top of the page. Select a title to show its details and images.
Announcements leave the list when they expire or when staff remove them.

## Reopen an issue

If the problem returns, select **Reopen issue** and give a reason.
The owner, history, and any award stay. The timer resumes from its earlier total.
You can reopen an issue while reporting is closed.

## Understand time awards

The jury can award time with a public explanation.
Staff then apply it to the contest clock and confirm that in Minsky.
Minsky never changes the contest clock itself.
